About Highway Vocational Center

Highway Vocational Center is a NITA registered institution committed to transforming lives by offering inclusive training opportunities that build skills, foster self-reliance, and promote economic development.

As a Center of Excellence, we provide quality training in Beauty and Therapy, HairDressing, Computer Packages, and Cyber & eCitizen Services. Our mission is to create a community where every individual has the opportunity to learn, grow, and achieve economic independence through skill development.
